Privacy Policy

Playa Bowls COGS Automation

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Purpose

Playa Bowls COGS Automation is an internal-use financial reporting application designed to consolidate authorized accounting and sales information for participating businesses.

2. Information Collected

The application may access authorized QuickBooks Online accounting information, including expense categories, cost of goods sold, and financial reporting data. It may also process Clover sales information.

3. Use of Information

Information is used solely for internal financial analysis, cost monitoring, and business reporting. The application is not offered as a public service.

4. Data Storage and Security

The application is intended to run on an authorized local computer. Financial reports may be stored in a restricted-access Microsoft OneDrive account. Authentication credentials and financial records are not published on this website.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not sell financial data. Data may be processed by authorized service providers necessary for accounting, reporting, authentication, and file storage.

6. Access and Revocation

Authorized administrators may disconnect the application from connected accounting services and revoke access through the applicable provider's account settings.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or data handling.
