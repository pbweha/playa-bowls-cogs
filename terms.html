End-User License Agreement

Playa Bowls COGS Automation

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Application Purpose

Playa Bowls COGS Automation is a private, internal-use financial reporting application designed to consolidate accounting and sales data for authorized businesses.

2. Authorized Use

The application is intended exclusively for use by its owner and authorized personnel for internal business reporting and analysis. It is not offered for public distribution.

3. Accounting Integrations

The application may connect to QuickBooks Online and other authorized business systems to retrieve financial information. Users must have appropriate authorization to access connected accounts.

4. Data Ownership

All financial and business data remains the property of the respective businesses. Use of the application does not transfer ownership of such information.

5. Application Availability

The application is provided for internal operational purposes and may be modified, suspended, or discontinued at the discretion of its owner.

6. Reporting Accuracy

Reports generated by the application are intended to support internal management decisions. Users are responsible for reviewing financial information and verifying its accuracy against the original accounting records.

7. Termination of Access

Authorized administrators may discontinue use of the application or revoke access to connected services at any time.

8. Changes to This Agreement

This agreement may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or operating requirements.
